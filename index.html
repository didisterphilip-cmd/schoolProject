TURRET DEFENSE★ KING ★

Hold the line across 100 escalating levels. Earn gold, build turrets, upgrade your arsenal, and unlock devastating new towers as you climb toward the crown.

Goal: Stop every enemy from crossing your base. Each leak costs lives — run out and you lose the level.
Build: Pick a turret from the sidebar, then click a grassy tile to place it (costs gold).
Earn: Every kill pays gold. Clearing a wave pays a bonus. Send waves early for extra gold.
Upgrade / Sell: Click a placed turret to upgrade it (up to Lv.4) or sell it for a refund.
Speed: Use 1x/2x/3x and Pause. Beat a level to unlock the next — and new turrets along the way!
An original tower-defense built with HTML5 Canvas — no downloads, plays right in your browser.
